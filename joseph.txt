Joseph was the eleventh son of Jacob in the Bible.
His father gave him a colorful coat, angering his brothers.
His brothers sold him into slavery in Egypt.
He worked in the house of Potiphar but was falsely accused.
Joseph interpreted dreams in prison, including Pharaoh's.
Pharaoh made him second-in-command over Egypt.
He stored grain during seven years of plenty.
His brothers came to Egypt during famine and did not recognize him.
Joseph eventually revealed himself and forgave his brothers.
His story is told in Genesis chapters 37–50.
